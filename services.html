Our Services

Comprehensive moving solutions, tailored to you.

Whether you're moving locally within Calgary or Edmonton, or relocating between the two, we have the expertise and equipment to make it seamless.

Residential Moving

We specialize in residential moves of all sizes, from studio apartments to large family homes. Our team takes special care to protect your furniture and personal belongings.

  • Local moves within Calgary and Edmonton
  • Inter-city moves between Calgary and Edmonton
  • Full packing and unpacking services available
  • Professional furniture assembly and disassembly

Commercial Moving

Moving a business requires precision, planning, and minimal disruption. We work around your schedule so operations continue smoothly.

  • Office relocations of any size
  • Minimal downtime planning and execution
  • IT equipment handling and setup
  • After-hours and weekend moves available

Packing & Unpacking

Let our professionals handle the packing! We use high-quality materials and proven techniques so your belongings arrive safely.

  • Full packing service with quality materials
  • Partial packing for fragile items
  • Custom crate building for special items
  • Unpacking and arrangement services

Furniture Assembly & Disassembly

We disassemble your furniture before the move and reassemble it at your new location, saving you time and hassle on moving day.

  • Professional disassembly and reassembly
  • Hardware and instruction management
  • Careful padding and protection
  • Quick and efficient service

Storage Solutions

Sometimes you need temporary or long-term storage. We offer secure, climate-controlled options to keep your belongings safe during transitions.

  • Short-term and long-term storage
  • Climate-controlled facilities
  • 24/7 security monitoring
  • Flexible rental periods

Junk Removal & Donation

Moving is a great time to declutter! We haul away what you no longer need and donate usable items to local charities.

  • Junk and debris removal
  • Donation coordination with local charities
  • Environmentally responsible disposal
  • Tax receipt documentation available
